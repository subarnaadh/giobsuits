The GioB Difference

Why Every GioB Suit Is 100% Australian Wool

A suit should never be polyester. It's the single fabric decision that determines how a suit feels on a 12-hour wedding day, how it ages after ten dry cleans, and what happens to it a decade from now. We build every suit and tuxedo from 100% Australian wool because there simply isn't a synthetic substitute that does the job as well.

🌬️

Breathable

Wool fiber is naturally porous — it moves air and wicks moisture instead of trapping heat against your skin the way polyester does. You stay comfortable through a full day on your feet.

🌡️

Temperature-Regulating

Wool insulates in the cold and releases heat in the warmth, so the same suit performs at a spring wedding and a winter gala. Polyester does neither — it just holds whatever temperature it's given.

🌿

Natural & Renewable

Wool is grown, not manufactured. It's a renewable fiber that biodegrades in soil within months. Polyester is a petroleum-based plastic that persists in landfills for centuries.

♻️

Lower Environmental Impact

Because wool is durable and biodegradable, wool garments are worn longer and don't shed the microplastics that synthetic fabrics release into water systems with every wash.

Wool vs. Polyester

Here's the side-by-side. This is why "100% wool" is on every single product page — it's not a marketing line, it's what you're actually wearing.

Property 100% Australian Wool Polyester
Breathability Naturally porous fiber, moves air freely Traps heat and moisture against the body
Temperature Regulation Insulates in cold, releases heat in warmth No regulating properties, feels the same year-round
Fiber Source Natural, renewable — grown on sheep Synthetic — derived from petroleum
Biodegradability Breaks down naturally in months Persists in landfills for centuries
Microplastic Shedding None — it's a natural fiber Sheds microplastics into water with every wash
Durability & Drape Holds shape, resists wrinkles, drapes cleanly Loses shape over time, characteristic synthetic sheen
Odor Resistance Naturally antimicrobial, resists odor Traps and retains odor over time
